Once upon a time, there was a cow who lived on a farm with her beloved calf. Every day, she would go to the forest to graze and return home in the evening to feed her calf. One day, while grazing, a hungry tiger caught the cow and intended to eat her for lunch. The cow, realizing her predicament, pleaded with the tiger to spare her life, explaining that her calf would die of hunger without her milk. She promised to return after feeding her calf, hoping to buy some time. 

The tiger, intrigued by the cow's sincerity, agreed to let her go but warned her to return quickly. The cow hurried home, fed her calf, and reassured it that she would be back soon. True to her word, she returned to the forest and called out to the tiger. The tiger was astonished to see her return, as he had expected her to flee and never come back. Impressed by her honesty and integrity, the tiger decided not to harm her and instead vowed to protect her from other dangers in the forest.
